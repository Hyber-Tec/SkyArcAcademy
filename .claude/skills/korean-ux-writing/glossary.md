# Sky Arc glossary (English → Korean)

One Korean word for one thing, on every screen, in the app, the website and notifications. Proper names stay as the
academy writes them: Sky Arc, the programs (BEGINNER · FOUNDATION), the levels (Marksman, Sharpshooter), SIUS.

| English | Korean | Notes |
|---|---|---|
