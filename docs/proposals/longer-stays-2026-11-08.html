A student who stays longer, and a canceled student who takes no space

“Is there any way you can make it so I can edit a student’s specific class hour. [A student] is coming in from 4–5:30, staying for 30 minutes longer because we have space in the next class as well.” The manager’s message. The people below are made up.

Two things to decide: how a student’s own hours work when they don’t match their class, and how a canceled student shows without filling a seat. Each part had three options; Goo picked A and 1 (docs/DECISIONS.md §38).

How it works today

A seat can already have its own time, but only inside its class.

That’s how a half-hour trial works (“Part of the class only” in Add a student). Families’ screens and the phone app already show a seat’s own time.

Nothing lets a seat run past its class, or changes its time once booked.

So the workaround is booking the student into the next class as well, which the screenshot shows: two seats, two marks, two classes for the family.

A canceled student keeps their place, struck through.

That copies the paper sheet. Their seat is free (the class says 4/6), but the card still fills a row, so only one open seat shows instead of two.

Part 1

A student stays longer, or comes earlier

The manager’s case: a student in the 4–5 PM class stays until 5:30, in a seat the 5 PM class isn’t using.

A

Stretch the student’s card

Goo’s pick

Drag the right edge of a student’s card to stay later, or the left edge to come earlier, in 15-minute steps. Their card reaches into the next class and holds a seat there until they leave. On a phone-sized screen, the card’s ⋯ menu has “Change hours” with two time pickers.

Try it: drag the handle on Ethan’s card to the right.

The office

  • + One visit stays one seat: marked once, canceled once.
  • + The 5 PM class counts the seat he holds, and the app stops the stretch when the next class is full at that time.
  • Just for that day: copying the week to the next books him for the usual hour.

Families

  • + They see one class, 4:00–5:30 PM, on the website and in the phone app they already have.
  • For a class still ahead, they get the usual “Class time changed” notice, in Korean too.

Phone app and work

  • + No phone build needed: the website changes when merged, and the installed app already shows “4–5:30” on his seat.
  • − Until the next phone build, the phone’s 5 PM class doesn’t count him.
  • One pull request.
B

Book the next class too, for part of it

Keep each seat inside its class. A “Stay longer” item in the card’s ⋯ menu books him into the 5 PM class from 5:00 to 5:30, and the two cards point to each other. This can already be done by hand today.

The office

  • + The smallest change: “Part of the class only” already does it.
  • − Two seats for one visit: marked twice, canceled twice, moved twice.

Families

  • − They see two classes that day, 4:00–5:00 and 5:00–5:30, and get two notices.

Phone app and work

  • + Counts are right on the installed phone app at once, since the seat really is in the 5 PM class.
  • A small pull request.
C

No fixed classes

Your first idea: drop the 2:30, 4 and 5 o’clock blocks. Each student becomes a bar from when they arrive to when they leave, on one of the six seats, and the usual class times are only faint guides behind them.

The office

  • + The most freedom: anyone, any time, dragged anywhere.
  • − Everything built on classes has to be rebuilt: families’ requests and makeups ask for a class, seat counts, “every coach in teaches the class”, Home and reports.

Families

  • − Asking for a makeup or another time needs a new way to pick a time.

Phone app and work

  • − Needs a new phone build for staff and families. Installed apps would show it wrong until people update.
  • − Weeks of work, right after the academy started using the app. A gets the manager’s case without it.
Part 2

A canceled student

A 2:30–4 PM class with six seats: four students coming, one canceled. Two seats are free.

1

Canceled names go under the seats

Goo’s pick

The seats show who’s coming and every open seat, so this class shows two. Under them, one short line names who canceled. Each name opens its menu: Restore the seat, Book a makeup.

Why

  • + One rule everywhere: seats are for who’s coming; canceled is listed below.
  • + It’s already where a refilled class lists its extra cancellations (“Also canceled”), so that becomes the only place.

Cost

  • A line of small text instead of a whole card.
  • Website at once; the phone’s class cards follow in the next phone build.
2

The freed seat says who canceled

The canceled card turns into an open seat in the same place: hatched, “Add a student”, with the canceled name small on top.

Why

  • + No extra line, and you see which seat freed up.

Cost

  • − Once someone takes that seat, the name has to move under the seats anyway, so canceled names end up in two places.
3

A count in the class’s header

“1 canceled” sits next to the seat count. Click it to see who and restore them.

Why

  • + The most compact.

Cost

  • − Who canceled is a click away. A coach glancing at the class won’t see the name.
