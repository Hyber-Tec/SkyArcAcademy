# TestFlight: "What to Test"

Paste into the TestFlight group's **What to Test** box (App Store Connect → TestFlight → the build → Test Details),
when the app goes to TestFlight ([docs/publishing.md](../publishing.md), part 2). Testers see it in the TestFlight app.
Fill in the ALL CAPS part.

```
Thanks for testing Sky Arc, the app of Sky Arc Sports Academy's office.

BEFORE YOU START
- Sign in with the email the academy has for you (Google, or email and password), as on the website.
- When Scan asks for the camera or your photos, allow it (it's only for paper targets).

PLEASE TRY
1. Today: are today's classes right, in the academy's time?
2. Schedule: another day and week; open a class and call or text its coach.
3. Scan: photograph a paper target flat and whole, or choose a photo of one. Are the scores right? Save it for the student, then find it in their files.
4. Students: search for a student; open their family's contacts, their classes, scores and a file.
5. More: the coaches, the appearance, the version.
6. Anything that looked wrong, was slow, or confused you.

HOW TO REPORT
In the TestFlight app: Sky Arc → Send Beta Feedback (you can attach a screenshot), or write to CONTACT EMAIL. Say what you did, what happened, and the time.
```
